# Paradise Innovations — Brand & Production Style Guide

## Identity
- **Brand:** PARADISE INNOVATIONS
- **Positioning:** "Driving Intelligent Government Transformation"
- **Tagline:** "INNOVATIONS THAT TRANSFORMS"
- **Logo:** use the existing logo asset from the project repository as-is — preserve its proportions and colors exactly; do not redraw, recolor, or distort it.

## Color Palette
| Role | Color | Suggested hex (adjust to match the actual logo file) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / dominant | Midnight Blue | `#0B1F3A` |
| Secondary | Royal Blue | `#1E4FA3` |
| Accent / highlight | Cyan | `#2FD3E8` |
| Neutral / text-on-dark | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Accent (sparing) | Subtle Gold | `#C9A646` |

Use gold sparingly — logo reveals, key stat callouts, chapter-title underlines. Never as a body-text color (contrast/legibility).

## Applied consistently to
- **Intro:** logo animation over midnight blue, cyan highlight sweep, gold accent on tagline reveal.
- **Outro:** mirrors the intro — same logo treatment, closing on midnight blue.
- **Chapter transitions:** full-bleed midnight-blue card, white Arabic chapter title, thin cyan underline rule.
- **Screen annotations / callouts:** royal-blue rounded rectangles with white text, cyan arrow/pointer for cursor highlights.
- **Lower thirds:** module technical name (small, cyan) above business name (larger, white), on a semi-transparent midnight-blue bar.
- **Watermark:** small Paradise Innovations wordmark, bottom-right, low-opacity, present throughout both videos.
- **Thumbnails:** midnight-blue background, one representative screen crop, bold white Arabic title, logo bottom-left.
- **Subtitles:** white text, thin dark outline/shadow for legibility over live screen recordings; bottom-center, max 2 lines.

## Screen-recording conventions
- Cursor highlighted with a soft cyan glow/circle on click.
- Zoom into the specific field/button being discussed before the narration names it — don't narrate something off-screen.
- Smooth crossfade (6–10 frames) between shots, not hard cuts, except at chapter boundaries (hard cut on the transition card).
- Keep the Odoo UI's own RTL Arabic layout untouched — do not mirror or flip the recording.

## What this guide does not cover
Actual color grading, motion-graphics templates, and a finished thumbnail/watermark asset were not produced in this pass — see `06_QA/Final_Delivery_Report.md` for why, and what an editor needs to build them from this guide.
